## 1.Purpose and objectives

Cadence FinancialGroup,Inc. (the "Group") accepts risk as an inherent consequence of providing investment banking,asset management and consumer banking services. This policy establishes the Group-wide framework through whichrisk is identified,assessed,treated,monitoredandreported,so thatrisk-taking isdeliberate,understood,andremains withinthe appetite setbythe Board of Directors.

This policy is the apex document of the Group risk policy hierarchy. All subordinate risk policies, standards and procedures must be consistent with it. Where a subordinate document conflicts with this policy, this policy prevails. Interpretation questions are directed to the policy author, Tobias Lindqvist (t.lindqvist@cadencefg.com, +44 20 7555 0171).

## 1.1 Objectives

1. Protect capital, liquidity and earnings by ensuring exposures remain within approved appetite and regulatory limits.

2. Enable informed risk-taking through a consistent, comparable view of risk across business lines and geographies.

3.Establishclear accountability foreverymaterial risk,with a single namedownerat anappropriate level of seniority.

4. Ensure regulatory compliance with prudential, conduct and data-protection obligations in each jurisdiction.

5.Protect customers and market integrityby embedding conduct considerations into riskdecisions.

6. Safeguard reputation by identifying and managing risks to stakeholder confidence.

7.Supportresilience so theGroup canabsorb severe but plausible shocks andcontinue important business services.

8.Promote a healthyrisk culture in which concerns are raised early,escalated withoutfear,and acted upon.

## 1.2 Principles

| PRINCIPLE | STATEMENT |
|---|---|
| Risk ownership | Risk is owned by the business that creates it.Ownership cannot be delegated to a control function or to a third party |
| Proportionality | The depth ofassessment, control andreporting is proportionate to the materiality and complexity oftherisk. |
| Independence | The second and third lines have direct, unrestricted access to the Board and are free from business pressure. |
| Transparency | Risks are reported completely and withoutfiltering.Understating or delaying a riskreport is a breachof this policy. |
| Forward focus | The framework considers emerging and prospective risks,not only realised losses and historical events. |
| Consistency | Acommon taxonomy, scoring scale anddefinitions applyGroup-wide so that risks aggregate meaningfully. |
| Substance over form | Assessmentsreflectactualexposureandactualcontrol operation,notdocumentedintent. |

## 2.Scope and applicability

## 2.1 Entities in scope

This policy applies to Cadence Financial Group, Inc. and every subsidiary, branch and representative office it controls, including regulated entities in the United States, the United Kingdom, the European Union, India, Singapore and Hong Kong. Where a subsidiary is subject to a local requirement more stringent than this policy, the local requirement applies and the divergence is recorded in the Group Policy Divergence Register maintained by Delphine R. Okonjo (d.okonjo@cadencefg.com).

Joint ventures and associates in which the Group does not hold control are out of scope. The Group's interest in such entities is managed as an investment exposure under the Credit Risk Policy and as a reputational exposure under this policy.

## 2.2 Persons in scope

This policy applies to all directors, employees, secondees, contingent workers and contractors acting for the Group, regardless of location or employment status. Third parties performing outsourced activities are bound through contractual terms requiring equivalence with this policy, per Section 18.3.
