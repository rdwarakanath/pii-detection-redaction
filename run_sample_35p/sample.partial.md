1. Purpose and objectives

Cadence Financial Group, Inc. (the "Group") accepts risk as sm inherent consequence of providing investment banking, asset management and consumer banking services. This policy establishes the Group-wide framework through which risk is identified, assessed, treated, monitored and reported, so that risk-taking is deliberate, understood, and remains within the appetite set by the Board of Directors.

This policy is the apex document of the Group risk policy hierarchy. All subordinate risk policies, standards and procedures must be consistent with it. Where a subordinate document conflicts with this policy, this policy prevails. Interpretation questions are directed to the policy author, Tobias Lindqvist (t.lindqvist@cadencefg.com, +44 20 7555 0171).

1.1 Objectives

1. Protect capital, liquidity and earnings by ensuring exposures remain within approved appetite and regulatory limits.
2. Enable informed risk-taking through a consistent, comparable view of risk across business lines and geographies.
3. Establish clear accountability for every material risk, with a single named owner at an appropriate level of seniority.
4. Ensure regulatory compliance with prudential, conduct and data-protection obligations in each jurisdiction.

5. Protect customers and market integrity by embedding conduct considerations into risk decisions.

6. Safeguard reputation by identifying and managing risks to stakeholder confidence.

7. Support resilience so the Group can absorb severe but plausible shocks and continue important business services.
8. Promote a healthy risk culture in which concerns are raised early, escalated without fear, and acted upon.

1.2 Principles

PRINCIPLE STATEMENT

Risk ownership Risk is owned by the business that creates it. Ownership cannot be delegated to a control function or to a third party. Proportionality The depth of assessment, control and reporting is proportionate to the materiality and complexity of the risk. Independence The second and third lines have direct, unrestricted access to the Board and are free from business pressure. Transparency Risks are reported completely and without filtering. Understating or delaying a risk report is a breach of this policy. Forward focus The framework considers emerging and prospective risks, not only realised losses and historical events. Consistency Acommon taxonomy, scoring scale and definitions apply Group-wide so that risks aggregate meaningfully. Substance over form Assessments reflect actual exposure and actual control operation, not documented intent.

3.3 Relationship to the internal control framework

Risk assessment determines what could go wrong and how severely; the control framework determines what is done to prevent or detect it. The two are linked through the control library, in which every control maps to at least one risk in the Group Risk Register, and every risk rated Elevated or above maps to at least one control.

3.4 Relationship to capital and liquidity planning

The output of risk identification and assessment is a direct input to the Internal Capital Adequacy Assessment Process (ICAAP) and the Internal Liquidity Adequacy Assessment Process (ILAAP). Material risks not adequately captured by regulatory capital requirements are assessed for Pillar 2 add-ons by the Group Treasurer, Marta Szewczyk (m.szewczyk@cadencefg.com), with challenge from the Group Chief Risk Officer.

3.5 Cross-border considerations

Each regulated subsidiary maintains a board-approved local addendum documenting the local regulatory overlay, the composition of the local risk committee and the delegated authorities of local management. Local addenda may narrow Group appetite but may never widen it. Cross-border data flows arising from Group-level risk aggregation — which include the employee identifiers and contact details reproduced throughout this policy — are assessed under the Data Privacy Policy before transfer.
